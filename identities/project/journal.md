# Project Journal

Append-only. Curation means trimming noise and re-tagging status, never
rewriting history: mark an entry superseded, do not delete it.

## Entry format
### YYYY-MM-DD HH:MMZ — task or decision title
Type: task | decision | method-note | correction | ritual
What: what you did or decided
Why: the reasoning
Evidence: files, sources, commands or review outputs that back it
Depends on: the project-memory assumptions or earlier entries it relies on
Confidence: low | medium | high
Status: in-progress | done | blocked | superseded-by <entry title>

Lesson (2026-10-12, objpron): an object-slot check whose object test lists nouns and plurals misses an accusative object pronoun, because a pronoun sits in neither the noun list nor the plural list, so the ungoverned object goes unseen in these fixtures and a false relative bound passes. The five gap fixtures (the fix kept them failed, the fix handed him failed, the fix kept us failed, the fix kept her failed, the fix told her failed) read accept pre-fix and refuse post-fix; reading the object slot as admitting a pronoun too refuses them. The one pronoun that is both object and determiner needed a shape rather than a list: "her" refuses the bound when the next word is a verb (told her failed, so her is the object), and stays accept when the next word is a noun read by shape (kept her gate held, so her opens the next clause subject), because a following singular common noun like "gate" sits outside the noun lists and a list-based test would flip that bounded accept to refuse.

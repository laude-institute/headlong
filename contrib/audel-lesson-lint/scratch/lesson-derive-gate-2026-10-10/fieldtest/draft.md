lesson: a number goes into a note or brief only after it is traced to the source extract, not to my memory of the source
Case (2026-10-10): I wrote 25% and 10.8% into my handoff as Opera body findings, and neither number exists in the paper body, the HTML, or my read note. A re-check against all three sources found zero hits and both were dropped as confabulated. The habit this encodes: before a numeric claim enters a note or a brief, grep the extract on file for it (or the numbers near it), and if the extract is not on file, write the claim as qualitative or drop it. Absence of the extract is not permission to remember the number.
